# Candidate 2 Research Notes

## Objective

Evaluate the Phishing Email Dataset for detecting phishing and email-based scams.

---

## Dataset Summary

This dataset combines multiple publicly available phishing email datasets into one comprehensive collection. It contains legitimate and phishing emails collected from various research sources.

---

## Original Datasets

- CEAS 2008
- Enron
- Ling
- Nazario
- Nigerian Fraud
- SpamAssassin

---

## Dataset Characteristics

- Approximately 82,500 emails
- Multiple CSV files
- One merged dataset
- Rich email metadata
- Suitable for NLP and cybersecurity research

---

## Common Features

- Sender
- Receiver
- Date
- Subject
- Email Body
- URLs
- Label

---

## Strengths

- Large-scale dataset
- Real-world phishing emails
- Multiple benchmark datasets combined
- Rich metadata
- Suitable for Explainable AI
- Supports phishing URL analysis
- Useful for email fraud detection

---

## Weaknesses

- Limited to email communication
- Does not include WhatsApp or Telegram chats
- Some datasets have different feature structures
- Requires preprocessing before merging

---

## Suitability for CyberShield-AI

Highly suitable for the Scam Communication AI module. It significantly improves the AI's understanding of phishing emails, credential theft attacks, fake invoices, and social engineering techniques.
