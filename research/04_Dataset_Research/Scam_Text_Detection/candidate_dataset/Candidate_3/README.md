## Purpose

Designed to detect scam conversations by learning behavioral and linguistic patterns rather than relying solely on topic-specific keywords.

---

## Original Data Sources

Conversation patterns were collected from publicly available victim reports on:

- Twitter
- Facebook
- Instagram
- YouTube
- Reddit
- Quora
- Blogs
- Other public discussion platforms

The collected conversations were anonymized and augmented to improve diversity while preserving realistic scam behavior.

---

## Why Consider This Dataset?

- Conversation-based analysis
- Based on real scam reports
- Supports Explainable AI
- Excellent for social engineering detection
- Useful for Scam Communication AI
