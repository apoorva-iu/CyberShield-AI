# Candidate 3 Research Notes

## Objective

Evaluate a conversation-based dataset for detecting scam communication through behavioral analysis.

---

## Dataset Summary

This dataset contains 800 telephone conversation transcripts divided equally between scam and legitimate conversations. Rather than focusing only on keywords, it emphasizes conversational behavior and persuasion techniques commonly used in scams.

---

## Dataset Statistics

- Total Conversations: 800
- Scam: 400
- Non-Scam: 400
- File Format: TXT
- Labels: Scam / Non-Scam

---

## Data Sources

Conversation patterns were derived from publicly available reports shared on social media, online forums, blogs, and victim experiences.

Researchers further diversified the dataset using controlled AI-based augmentation while maintaining realistic scam patterns.

---

## Strengths

- Conversation-based learning
- Real behavioral scam patterns
- Social engineering focus
- Explainable AI compatible
- Balanced dataset
- Published IEEE research

---

## Weaknesses

- Smaller than other candidates
- Telephone conversation focus
- Requires preprocessing of TXT files

---

## Suitability for CyberShield-AI

This dataset complements the Financial Scam Dataset and Phishing Email Dataset by teaching the AI to recognize conversational manipulation techniques used across phone calls, messaging applications, and social media platforms.

---

## Current Decision

Selected as Candidate 3.
