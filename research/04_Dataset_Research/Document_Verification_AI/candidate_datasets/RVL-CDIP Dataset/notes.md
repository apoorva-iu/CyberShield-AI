## Why Selected for CyberShield-AI

The RVL-CDIP Dataset has been selected as Candidate 2 because it enables document classification and layout understanding before OCR and authenticity verification. It complements the OCR Dataset of Multi-Type Documents by allowing CyberShield-AI to first identify the document category and structure, thereby improving the overall document verification pipeline.
