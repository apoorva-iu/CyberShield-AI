# Candidate 2 - Multimodal Real/Fake Job Posting Prediction

## Purpose

This folder contains the research and evaluation of the Multimodal Real/Fake Job Posting Prediction dataset as Candidate 2 for the Fake Job Detection module of CyberShield-AI.

This dataset extends the original EMSCAD dataset by providing company website screenshots in addition to textual and structured job information.

## Research Goal

The objective of this evaluation is to determine whether the additional visual information improves fake job detection compared to the original EMSCAD dataset.
