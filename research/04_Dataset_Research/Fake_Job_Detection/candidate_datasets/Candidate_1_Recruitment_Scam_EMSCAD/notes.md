## What is EMSCAD?

EMSCAD (Employment Scam Aegean Dataset) is a publicly available benchmark dataset developed by researchers at the University of the Aegean for detecting fraudulent online job advertisements. It contains both legitimate and fraudulent job postings with textual and structured features, making it widely used in machine learning and natural language processing research for fake job detection.
