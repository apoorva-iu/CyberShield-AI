# Problem Statement

Cyber fraud is rapidly increasing across digital platforms. Students, job seekers, and professionals are frequently targeted through fake job postings, phishing websites, scam messages, forged documents, and fake recruiter communications.

Existing verification platforms mainly focus on storing documents securely but do not provide intelligent fraud detection or explainable risk analysis.

There is a need for an integrated platform capable of analyzing multiple types of cyber fraud using Artificial Intelligence while maintaining tamper-proof verification records through blockchain technology.

CyberShield AI aims to solve this problem by combining AI, OCR, explainable decision-making, and blockchain into a unified cybersecurity platform.
